1. Tell us about your supplier work

This calculator is about the work you send to third-party contractors and specialists, and the calls you have with them. Most calculators price a single call. Supplier work costs more than the calls. This one starts with those supplier calls, because that is where the money is easiest to see: build a typical call person by person and watch the cost appear. Then it adds up four things: the calls and the prep around them, the briefing, chasing and checking, the rework when something comes back wrong, and the delay while you wait. Use rough numbers if you do not have exact ones. The start figures are examples, so replace them with yours.
